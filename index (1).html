About Our Club

Rising Stars Club is a home for aspiring actors. Members train in acting, voice and stage presence, study great films and perform together.

Our goal is to build confident performers who can tell stories on screen and stage, support their castmates and share their work with the community.

What We Do

Acting Workshops

Voice, movement, emotion and character work, from first lines to full scenes.

Short Film Productions

Write, cast, shoot and edit our own movies as a team, on and off camera.

Auditions and Screenings

Practice real auditions, then watch and discuss films together.
